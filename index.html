I'm an engineering undergrad at the University of Moratuwa working on computer vision and deep learning, with a particular interest in vision-language models, autonomous systems and world models.

Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of computer vision, multimodal learning and autonomous systems. I am interested in models that understand the world through vision, language, audio and other signals; in world models that let a model predict outcomes and plan before it acts; and in bringing these methods to autonomous machines that must perceive, reason and decide reliably in the real world.
